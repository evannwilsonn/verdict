Verdict

Did they like it?

Hand in any movie review. A DistilBERT model reads it in your browser, stamps a verdict on it, and marks up the sentences that decided it. Never trained on IMDB, it gets – of 25,000 IMDB reviews right on its own, and – with a small word-counting model trained on IMDB beside it.

Marked up

Each sentence is taken out in turn and the review is read again. The pen shows how far the verdict moved without it.

ColophonIssue 07 of Evan Wilson's AI project run. Source and write-up.

Set in Gloock, IBM Plex Serif and Nothing You Could Do, on newsprint. Two pens: green for "liked it", red for "didn't". Printed with TypeScript and Transformers.js; the model runs in your browser and nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

Reviews from the Large Movie Review Dataset: Andrew L. Maas, Raymond E. Daly, Peter T. Pham, Dan Huang, Andrew Y. Ng and Christopher Potts, "Learning Word Vectors for Sentiment Analysis", ACL 2011. Model: DistilBERT fine-tuned on SST-2 (Apache 2.0). The sample reviews were written for this page.
